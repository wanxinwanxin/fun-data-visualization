Each room takes one public data set that was easy to find and hard to use, and turns it into motion and sound. Every room has its own visual language, from a radar scope to a nautical chart.

Open a room, then click once to enter with sound. Use the chip at the bottom left to move between rooms.

Built from public data only. Each room documents its source, its sampling rule, and its sound mapping in a README.
